# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Focus Tracker to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience level, background or identity. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for all.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and first-time contributors.
- Assume good intent; ask questions before jumping to conclusions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code, not people.
- Respect differing opinions, experiences and time zones.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments about anyone's identity or background
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer [@somcpp](https://github.com/somcpp) on GitHub, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. Reports will be handled promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is harmful.
